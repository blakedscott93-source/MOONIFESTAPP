## Executive Summary

Based on comprehensive competitor analysis (Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer, ThinkUp, I Am, Manifestation Journal), Moonifest will launch with a **freemium model** offering 25-33% free content with ads, and a premium subscription at **$9.99/month or $49.99/year** (58% discount).

**Key Decision**: Start with generous free tier + 7-day trial to build user base, then optimize conversion through paywall during onboarding.

## 🎁 Free Trial Strategy

### 7-Day Free Trial (All Plans)

**What Users Get**:
- Full access to ALL premium features for 7 days
- No credit card required until trial ends
- Cancel anytime during trial (no charge)

**Trial Flow**:
1. **Day 1**: Onboarding → Goal selection → **Paywall shown** → Start 7-day trial

5. **Day 7**: Trial ends → Subscription starts OR downgrade to free tier

**Conversion Target**: 12-15% (industry benchmark from Headspace)

### Alternative: 14-Day Trial for Annual Plan

**For annual subscribers only**:

- Justifies bigger commitment
- Higher quality conversions (less refund requests)

**Messaging**: "Try annual plan risk-free for 14 days"

## 💳 Paywall Strategy

### Timing (Critical for Conversion)

**Primary Paywall**: During Onboarding (HIGHEST CONVERSION)
- **When**: After user selects goals (slide 3-4 of onboarding)
- **Why**: Research shows 2x conversion when paywall shown during onboarding vs. later
- **CTA**: "Start Your 7-Day Free Trial"
- **Messaging**:
  - "Unlock your personalized manifestation plan"
  - "Join 10,000+ manifestors transforming their lives"
  - "Full access for 7 days, then $4.17/month"

**Secondary Paywall**: Soft Sell (NON-INTRUSIVE)
- **When**: After completing first free affirmation/meditation
- **Why**: User experienced value, more likely to upgrade
- **CTA**: "Unlock 6 More Affirmation Sessions"
- **Messaging**:
  - "Loved 'Gain Balance'? Get 7 more sessions"

## 📱 Ad Monetization (Free Tier)

### Ad Network
**Primary**: Google AdMob
- Free platform by Google
- Multiple formats (native, rewarded, banner, interstitial)
- Best for wellness apps
- Easy integration

### Ad Placements

| Placement | Format | Frequency | User Impact | Revenue Potential |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Daily Spin** | Rewarded Video | Optional (watch for 2x rewards) | Low (user choice) | High (CPM $3-8) |
| **Affirmation List** | Native Ads | Every 4-5 items | Medium (blends in) | Medium (CPM $1-3) |
| **Home Screen** | Banner | Persistent bottom banner | Low (easy to ignore) | Low (CPM $0.50-1) |
| **Post-Session** | Interstitial | Max 1-2x per day | Medium-High | High (CPM $4-10) |

### Expected Revenue
- **Per Free User**: $0.50-$2.00/month (depending on engagement and geography)
- **US Users**: ~3x revenue vs. other regions
- **Daily Active User (DAU)**: $0.05-0.10 per day

### Ad Experience Guidelines
1. ❌ Never show ads during meditation/affirmation playback
2. ✅ Use rewarded ads for gamification (daily spin, extra features)
3. ✅ Frequency cap interstitials (max 2 per day)
4. ✅ Native ads should match app design
5. ✅ Clear "Upgrade to remove ads" CTA

## 💡 Key Recommendations

### Do's ✅

1. **Show paywall during onboarding** - Research proves 2x conversion vs. later
2. **Emphasize annual plan** - Higher LTV, lower churn, better for users
3. **Make free tier valuable** - Gratitude journal keeps users engaged daily
4. **Use rewarded ads** - Aligns with gamification (daily spin)
5. **Communicate value clearly** - "Ad-free + All content" is strongest message
6. **Offer trial immediately** - Remove friction, build trust
7. **Track everything** - Data-driven decisions on pricing/features
8. **Keep updating** - Add new content monthly for retention

### Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't offer 45-day trial** - Too long, delays revenue, high drop-off
2. **Don't gate journal/vision board completely** - These drive daily engagement
3. **Don't show ads during sessions** - Ruins experience, high churn
4. **Don't overcomplicate pricing** - 2-3 tiers max (monthly/annual/lifetime)
5. **Don't hide trial in fine print** - Make it obvious and easy
6. **Don't ignore free users** - They're word-of-mouth marketers
7. **Don't change pricing frequently** - Confuses users, erodes trust
8. **Don't launch without restore purchases** - Required by Apple/Google
