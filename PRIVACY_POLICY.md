# Privacy Policy for Moonifest

**Last Updated:** December 24, 2024

## Introduction

Moonifest ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Moonifest mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- **Gratitude Journal Entries**: Text and voice recordings you create
- **Affirmations**: Custom affirmations you write
- **Vision Board Images**: Photos you upload to your vision board
- **Tasks**: Must-do tasks you create
- **Mood Check-ins**: Your mood and energy level selections
- **Goal Preferences**: Your selected manifestation goals (wealth, love, health, career, happiness, spirituality)

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: App features you use, session duration, navigation patterns
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Performance Data**: App crashes, errors, and performance metrics (via Sentry)
- **Progress Data**: Streaks, completion statistics, glow points earned

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do NOT collect your name, email, or phone number (unless you provide it for support)
- We do NOT access your contacts, camera roll (except images you explicitly select), or location
- We do NOT share your personal data with third parties for advertising

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide App Functionality**: Store and sync your journal entries, affirmations, vision board, and progress
- **Track Progress**: Calculate streaks, completion rates, and glow points
- **Improve the App**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
- **Personalize Experience**: Tailor content based on your selected goals
- **Send Notifications**: Remind you of daily practices (if you enable notifications)
- **Provide Support**: Respond to your inquiries and troubleshoot issues

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Most data is stored **locally on your device** using encrypted storage
- You control your data and can delete it anytime from the app settings

### Cloud Sync (Optional)
- If enabled, we sync your data to Supabase (our secure database provider)
- All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
- Data at rest is encrypted in Supabase databases

### Security Measures
- Industry-standard encryption for data transmission
- Secure authentication for cloud sync
- Regular security audits and updates
- No plain-text storage of sensitive information

## Voice Recordings

- Voice journal recordings are stored locally on your device
- If you enable transcription, audio may be sent to our transcription service (OpenAI Whisper)
- Transcription services process audio temporarily and do not store it permanently
- You can disable voice transcription in settings

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

### Supabase (Database & Auth)
- **Purpose**: Optional cloud sync of your data
- **Data Shared**: Journal entries, affirmations, progress data (only if you enable sync)
- **Privacy Policy**: https://supabase.com/privacy

### Sentry (Error Tracking)
- **Purpose**: Crash reporting and performance monitoring
- **Data Shared**: Anonymous error logs, device info, app version
- **Privacy Policy**: https://sentry.io/privacy/

### OpenAI (Voice Transcription - Optional)
- **Purpose**: Convert voice recordings to text
- **Data Shared**: Audio recordings (processed and deleted immediately)
- **Privacy Policy**: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

### Expo (App Platform)
- **Purpose**: App development and push notifications
- **Data Shared**: Device tokens for notifications
- **Privacy Policy**: https://expo.dev/privacy

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access Your Data**: View all data stored in the app
- **Delete Your Data**: Clear all data from settings
- **Export Your Data**: Download your journal entries and progress (feature coming soon)
- **Opt-Out of Analytics**: Disable error tracking in settings
- **Disable Sync**: Use the app entirely offline
- **Control Notifications**: Enable/disable reminders anytime

## Children's Privacy

Moonifest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Data Retention

- **Active Users**: Data is retained as long as you use the app
- **Inactive Users**: If you stop using the app, data remains on your device until you delete it
- **Cloud Data**: Synced data is retained until you delete your account
- **Deleted Data**: Permanently deleted within 30 days of deletion request

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:
- In-app notification
- Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## International Data Transfers

If you use Moonifest outside the United States, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States where our servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we do NOT sell your data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

## GDPR Rights (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under GDPR:
- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
